Decision Choose a strategy

Question 1 — Vaccination. With a limited budget, vaccination is the better choice: it costs about 75% less overall (5.05M vs 20.15M EUR discounted) and has a much higher benefit-cost ratio (6.72 vs 3.26), meaning every euro spent goes further.
Question 2 — Test-and-slaughter. If budget isn't a constraint, test-and-slaughter wins on absolute impact: its NPV (45.53M EUR) is substantially larger than vaccination's (28.88M EUR), because it drives prevalence down faster and generates more total avoided losses.

The two questions have different correct answers on purpose — "best" strategy depends on the decision criterion, which is exactly the tension the original study highlights (vaccination wins on efficiency and cost; test-and-slaughter wins on total benefit and NPV).
