Cost-Benefit Analysis: A 6-Year Vaccination Investment

Build the full benefit, cost, and discounted cash flow table for a pig herd vaccination program, then check your work.

Learning objective: practice building a multi-year cost-benefit table with discounting — Net Present Value (NPV) and Cost-Benefit Ratio (CBR) — for an animal health investment.

Scenario: Golden Field Farm

Golden Field Farm raises 1,000 finishing pigs per cycle and loses money every year to Enzootic Pneumonia: deaths, slower growth, and antibiotic treatment. The farm is considering a whole-herd vaccination program. Year 0 is the setup year (buying a vaccine fridge and training staff); the program then runs for six years. The avoided-loss figures below already account for the vaccine's efficacy, so you can take them as given and focus on building the cost-benefit table.

Benefits — losses avoided each year (Years 1–6)

  • Avoided mortality loss: 180,000 THB/yr
  • Avoided growth/performance loss: 48,000 THB/yr
  • Avoided treatment cost: 9,600 THB/yr

Costs of the vaccination program

  • Capital cost (fridge, syringes, training): 15,000 THB, Year 0 only
  • Fixed cost (annual supervision/records): 2,000 THB/yr, Years 1–6
  • Variable cost (vaccine + labor): 73,000 THB/yr, Years 1–6
Interest rate used for discounting: r = 6% per year (given)
