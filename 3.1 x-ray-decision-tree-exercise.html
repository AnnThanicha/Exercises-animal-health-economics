A decision tree compares choices when the outcome is uncertain. At each chance node (●), something happens with a certain probability, and each result carries a cost. The expected cost of a chance node is found by multiplying each branch's probability by its cost, then adding the results:

expected cost = (P₁ × cost₁) + (P₂ × cost₂)
