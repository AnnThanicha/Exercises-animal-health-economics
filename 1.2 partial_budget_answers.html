Partial Budget Exercise — Answer Key

Corresponds to the three practice scenarios in partial_budget_exercise.html. The worked example lives separately in partial_budget_example.html and is not repeated here.

Scenario 1: Selective Dry Cow Therapy

  • Cow split under SDCT: 90 × 55% = 49.5 ≈ 50 cows high-SCC (antibiotic); 40 cows low-SCC (sealant only).
  • Cost under blanket therapy: 90 × 180 = 16,200 THB
  • Cost under SDCT: (50 × 180) + (40 × 90) = 9,000 + 3,600 = 12,600 THB → reduced cost (B) = 16,200 − 12,600 = 3,600 THB
  • Added SCC testing cost (D): 90 × 40 = 3,600 THB
  • Added mastitis cost (D): 2 × 3,200 = 6,400 THB
Net change = (0 + 3,600) − (0 + 3,600 + 6,400) = 3,600 − 10,000 = −6,400 THB/year
Recommendation: Do not adopt as designed — added mastitis and testing costs outweigh drug savings.

Discussion point for class: SDCT is usually still recommended in practice for antimicrobial stewardship reasons that a narrow financial partial budget doesn't capture — good prompt for discussing the limits of this tool.

Scenario 2: Automated Calf Feeder

  • Lease fee (D): 60,000 THB/year (given directly)
  • Maintenance/electricity (D): 12,000 THB/year
  • Labor saved (B): manual = 1.5 hr × 80 × 365 = 43,800 THB/year; automated = (20/60) hr × 80 × 365 = 9,733 THB/year → savings ≈ 34,067 THB/year
  • Added revenue from faster weaning weight gain (A): 40 calves × 4 kg × 65 THB = 10,400 THB/year
Net change = (10,400 + 34,067) − (0 + 60,000 + 12,000) = 44,467 − 72,000 = −27,533 THB/year
Recommendation: Do not adopt at this scale/price — labor and weight-gain benefits do not cover the lease and maintenance cost.

Sensitivity check: raising maintenance by 50% (to 18,000) worsens the loss further (net ≈ −33,533 THB); it does not change the recommendation, since the investment is already unprofitable at the base estimate.

Scenario 3: Herd Vaccination

  • Expected annual abortion cost without vaccination: (1/5) × 18 × 22,000 = 79,200 THB/year
  • Expected annual abortion cost with vaccination: (1/5) × (18 × 0.20) × 22,000 = 15,840 THB/year
  • Reduced cost (B): 79,200 − 15,840 = 63,360 THB/year
  • Vaccination cost (D): 120 × (220 + 15) = 28,200 THB/year
Net change = (0 + 63,360) − (0 + 28,200) = +35,160 THB/year
Recommendation: Vaccinate — expected savings substantially exceed the annual vaccine program cost.

Discussion (1-in-10-year scenario): expected cost without vaccination halves to ≈39,600 THB/year, and with vaccination to ≈7,920 THB/year; reduced cost (B) becomes ≈31,680 THB — still above the 28,200 THB vaccination cost, so the recommendation likely still holds, but the margin is much narrower. Good point for discussing sensitivity to disease-probability estimates.
